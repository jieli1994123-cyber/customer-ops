# Playbook: online stores (e-commerce)

## Who they are (in findable terms)

Independent stores selling physical products online — own storefront (Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, Squarespace) rather than marketplace-only sellers. Live store, real catalog, signs of actual trading.

## Where they are publicly listed

- Store directories & showcases: platform showcase pages, "built on" galleries, niche store directories
- App ecosystems: Shopify App Store **reviews** — store owners publicly name their store while reviewing (or complaining about) apps. Pain, in public, with a store name attached
- Communities: platform forums, Reddit communities for store owners, Facebook-group alternatives that are publicly indexed
- Social storefronts: Instagram/TikTok shops with a link to an own store

## Search queries that work

- `site:apps.shopify.com <incumbent app> reviews` — then follow 1–3★ reviews mentioning the pain the user's product solves
- `"<category>" "powered by shopify" store` / equivalent for other platforms
- `"<product category>" store "wholesale" OR "our story"` — finds independents with an About page (and usually a named owner)
- Community search: `"<pain phrase>" shopify` on Reddit/forums

## Qualifying signals (feed /score)

- Catalog depth (20+ products suggests real volume), recent product additions
- Sells in the user's serviceable geography/language
- Uses the incumbent the user replaces — or visibly does the task manually (e.g. spreadsheet mentions, "DM us to order")

## Timing signals

- Public complaint about an incumbent app's price/reliability (`pain-public` — the strongest signal in this playbook)
- Store redesign / platform migration announced
- Seasonal peak approaching for their category (gift, swim, back-to-school…)
- New sales channel opened (wholesale page, second storefront)

## Disqualifiers (drop at discovery)

- Marketplace-only (Amazon/eBay storefront, no own site)
- Dropship-only with no brand/About page (no owner to talk to, churn city)
- Store dormant: no new products/posts in 6+ months
- Enterprise brand or chain (different universe of buyer)

## Contact patterns

- Owner is the buyer. Find the name on the About/Our Story page, press mentions, or podcast interviews
- Contact points: site contact page and published support email are public but generic — prefer a named owner + published email; Instagram DM is a `social` contact (manual only)
