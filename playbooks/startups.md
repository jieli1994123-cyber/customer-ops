# Playbook: early-stage startups

## Who they are (in findable terms)

Post-launch startups with a live product and a small team (roughly 2–50 people), ideally with a recent launch, raise, or hiring push. Pre-product idea-stage projects are out — no budget, no pain yet.

## Where they are publicly listed

- Launch platforms: Product Hunt (daily launches + topic pages), BetaList, Uneed, Fazier
- Startup directories: YC company directory (public), StartupBase, F6S, Wellfound company pages
- Communities: Hacker News (Show HN + "Who is hiring" threads), Indie Hackers product pages, X launch posts
- Registries of motion: Product Hunt "ship" pages and GitHub trending repos with a commercial product attached

## Search queries that work

- `site:producthunt.com <category keywords>` — recent launches in the space
- `site:news.ycombinator.com "Show HN" <category>` — technical founders showing working products
- `"<category>" startup "we're hiring" <role the product serves>` — hiring as a timing signal
- `site:indiehackers.com <category>` — solo/small teams with revenue talk

## Qualifying signals (feed /score)

- Live product with pricing or a waitlist converting to paid
- Team size signals on their site (About page, LinkedIn headcount in search snippets)
- They sell to a reachable market themselves (they'll understand outreach)

## Timing signals

- Launched in the last 90 days (building their first growth motions now)
- Announced a raise in the last 6 months
- First sales/ops/growth hire posted — the classic "founder can't do it all anymore" moment
- Public complaint about an incumbent tool on X/HN/Reddit (`pain-public`)

## Disqualifiers (drop at discovery)

- No live product (landing page only, "coming soon" older than 6 months)
- Stealth with no public footprint
- Direct competitor of the user
- Agency/consultancy masquerading as a product startup (different buyer)

## Contact patterns

- Founder is the buyer until ~15 staff; find them on the launch post, About page, or their public X/LinkedIn (profile only — no automation)
- Published contact: site contact page, launch-platform maker profile, `hello@`/`founder@` published on their site = `published`; anything else is a guess and gets labeled
