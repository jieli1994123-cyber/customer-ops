# Playbook: creators & media operators

## Who they are (in findable terms)

Independent creators and small media businesses: YouTubers, podcasters, newsletter writers, course creators — with a real audience (roughly 10k+ subscribers/followers or clear monetization) and a business behind the content (sponsors, products, memberships).

## Where they are publicly listed

- Platform pages themselves: YouTube About pages (public business-email gate is behind login — note it, never automate it), podcast show notes, newsletter archive/about pages (Substack, beehiiv, ConvertKit directories)
- Creator directories & marketplaces: sponsorship marketplaces' public listings, podcast guest directories, newsletter recommendation networks
- Communities: creator Discords/Slacks with public member pages, X lists, Indie Hackers for info-product sellers

## Search queries that work

- `site:youtube.com/@ <niche> "business inquiries"` in search snippets — surfaces channels that publish a contact route
- `"<niche>" newsletter "subscribe" "written by"` — solo/small-team publications
- `site:substack.com <niche> "about"` — publications with About pages naming the author
- `"<niche>" podcast "sponsored by"` — shows already monetizing (they buy services)

## Qualifying signals (feed /score)

- Consistent publishing cadence in the last 60 days
- Monetization present: sponsors, paid tier, products, courses
- Small team signals (they credit an editor/producer — a team means budget and delegation pain)

## Timing signals

- Public complaint about a tool/process (editing, sponsorship admin, audience analytics…) (`pain-public`)
- Milestone posts (100k subs, new season, new product launch)
- Hiring an editor/manager/ops help — posted publicly
- Just switched platforms (migration pain is buying pain)

## Disqualifiers (drop at discovery)

- Hobby cadence (no posts in 90+ days) or no monetization at all
- Media-network channels (owned by a network — buyer is a corporation, not the creator)
- Audience/segment mismatch with the user's product declared in ICP

## Contact patterns

- The creator is the business. Contact via: published business email on About/sponsorship pages (`published`), their own site's contact page, or public social profile (`social`, manual DM only)
- Managers/agents appear at the top end — if a public manager is listed, they are the right first contact and usually publish a business email
