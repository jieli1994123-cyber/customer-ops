# Playbook: products about to launch

## Who they are (in findable terms)

Products with a public launch moment in the next 0–60 days — or one in the last 7 days with more launches queued (a second platform, an app-store release, a 2.0). The buyer is almost always the founder/maker, publicly named on the launch itself.

## Where they are publicly listed

- Product Hunt: today's launches, and crucially the **pre-launch posts** makers publish 1–7 days before ("launching tomorrow, ask me anything")
- BetaList: today's/yesterday's listings (thin data — always resolve to the product's own site before judging)
- Launch newsletters & communities: launch-announcement posts on Indie Hackers, X ("launching next week"), maker blogs/Medium
- Crowdfunding **pre-launch** pages (Kickstarter/Indiegogo "coming soon") — the window closes the day the campaign goes live
- App-store "coming soon"/pre-order pages and TestFlight announcements

## Search queries that work

- `site:producthunt.com "launches" OR "launching" <category>` + date-bounded searches for launch posts
- `"<product category>" "launching on Product Hunt" <month>` — makers announce ahead on their own blogs
- `site:betalist.com <category>` for the daily listing flow
- Kickstarter/Indiegogo discovery filtered to not-yet-launched projects in the category

## Timing rules (this playbook lives or dies by them)

- **Pre-launch (T-30 to T-1)**: the golden window. Assets are being assembled *now*.
- **Launch day**: too late for the launch asset itself — pivot the angle to the *next* surface (app-store release, Product Hunt re-launch, ads) or to their next product if they're a serial maker.
- **Post-launch (T+1 to T+7)**: only viable with a concrete next milestone (another platform in review, a scheduled 2.0, a crowdfunding campaign following a PH launch).
- **Crowdfunding after go-live**: skip. The hero video necessarily exists; the buying window was the pre-launch page.

## Qualifying signals (feed /score)

- Named maker/founder on the launch post or product page (contact-ability lives here)
- Serial maker: launched before → will launch again → subscription-shaped buyer
- A second launch surface visibly pending ("iOS app in review", "Android coming soon")
- **Has-video check (mandatory):** open the launch page in a browser and look at the media gallery. If a polished launch video already leads the page, Fit for a first-video offer drops by 2 — pivot angle or skip. Text-only fetching cannot see this; do not guess it.

## Disqualifiers (drop at discovery)

- Launch already happened > 14 days ago with no next milestone
- Stealth/unnamed team with no public maker
- The product *is* a launch/marketing-asset tool (competitor or self-sufficient)

## Contact patterns

- Makers publish themselves: PH maker profile, launch-post bylines, personal blogs, X — all public channels
- Published business email appears on the product site's contact/press page about half the time in practice; when absent, the honest route is a public social touch first, not a guessed email
