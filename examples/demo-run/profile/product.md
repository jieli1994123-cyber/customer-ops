# Product profile — CartBack (FICTIONAL demo)

## One-liner
CartBack recovers abandoned Shopify carts with a two-message WhatsApp flow the store owner sets up in 10 minutes.

## What it does
- Detects abandoned checkouts -> sends a WhatsApp reminder at +1h with a one-tap resume link (saves the owner manual DM chasing)
- Second nudge at +24h with an optional owner-set discount code
- Weekly recovery report in plain language (recovered orders $, not vanity metrics)

## Who it's for (summary)
Independent Shopify stores selling physical goods, doing enough volume that abandoned carts hurt (roughly 200+ orders/mo).

## Pricing
Starter $29/mo, Growth $79/mo (published on the site).

## Proof
Landing page quotes 3 store owners by first name + store (user-provided during /setup confirmation).

## Alternatives customers use today
Shopify's built-in abandoned-checkout email; SMS apps; doing nothing (the majority).

## Hard no's
Marketplace-only sellers; stores under ~50 orders/mo (can't feel the pain yet); direct competitors in cart recovery.
